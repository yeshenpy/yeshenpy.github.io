About

I am a Tenured Associate Professor in the School of Software at Tianjin University (TJU). During my Ph.D. studies, I was advised by Prof. Jianye Hao. I received my Bachelor's degree from Tianjin University as well. My research mainly focuses on Reinforcement Learning, AI Agents, and Embodied AI.

I firmly believe in reinforcement learning and regard reinforcement as the core of intelligence. I am currently committed to advancing intelligent agents and embodied intelligence through reinforcement learning.
Open Positions
I am always looking for motivated students and researchers to join the group. All positions involve an interview process. More information is available on our team website.
  1. M.S. students: self-motivated, eager to learn, and willing to communicate and collaborate.
  2. Ph.D. students: applicants with a solid research foundation, preferably with one or more publications in top-tier venues.
  3. Postdoctoral researchers and research assistants: opportunities are available and can be discussed in detail.
  4. Research interns, research assistants, and postdoctoral researchers: positions are available. Please feel free to contact me if you are interested.
